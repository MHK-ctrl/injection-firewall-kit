## Read this before quoting the numbers

The corpus is small, synthetic and written by the same people who wrote the detectors. High recall here means the rules cover **these** phrasings; it says nothing about a semantically paraphrased attack in a language the corpus does not cover. Treat this as a regression test, not as a security rating. See `docs/06-limitations-and-honesty.md`.
